What we audit

Every surcharge class carriers hope you won't check

SLA

Late Delivery / SLA Failures

Money-back-guaranteed services that arrived late — automatically claimed on your behalf.

DIM

Dimensional (DIM) Weight Recalculations

Billed weights that don't match the actual package dimensions.

ADR

Invalid Address Correction Surcharges

Address correction fees applied to addresses that were correct all along.

DUP

Duplicate Tracking / Voided Label Charges

Labels billed twice, or charged after being voided and never used.

RES

Wrongful Residential Surcharges

Residential delivery fees applied to verified commercial addresses.

DISC

Unapplied Negotiated Discounts

Contracted rates and earned incentives that never made it onto the invoice.
